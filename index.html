Dr GrokBot KP

Personal health-records assistant for Jacob Hoffman. It requests read access to his own Kaiser Permanente chart through Epic SMART on FHIR so medications, conditions, allergies, labs, and visit notes can be tracked and visits can be prepared. It is not a clinician and does not provide medical advice.

Terms

  • Use is limited to the account holder (Jacob Hoffman). No one else is authorized unless he adds them later.
  • Chart data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared for marketing.
  • Tokens stay in his private keychain. Clinical summaries stay in his private notes.
  • He can revoke access in MyChart at any time. Data already stored is deleted when he asks.
  • No research use without a separate consent.

Public documentation: this page is the description of the app. There is no separate product website.
